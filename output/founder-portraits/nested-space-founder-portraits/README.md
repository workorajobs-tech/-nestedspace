# Nested Space founder portraits

Two matched portraits for the planned About page. Image 01 corresponds to PHOTO-2026-09-12-11-23-44.jpg; image 02 corresponds to 5AD47096-54E0-4E51-911E-6DB440EF6AC1.jpg. Names are intentionally not inferred from the photographs.

- Format: 1200 × 1500 pixels, 4:5 portrait.
- PNG masters: founder-01-cosmic-v1.png and founder-02-cosmic-v1.png in this folder.
- Website assets: public/images/founders/founder-01-cosmic-v1.webp and founder-02-cosmic-v1.webp.
- Use a 4:5 image frame to preserve the intended composition. No names or text are baked into the images.

Art direction: deep-space plum (#090419), violet (#7c3aed), soft pink and peach light, restrained orbital lines and stars, natural photographic detail, subtle grain, and a fade at the base. The colors follow the existing SpaceBackground.css theme.

These are direct edits of the supplied photographs: person segmentation, background replacement, matched crops, tonal mapping, and grain. Facial geometry and expressions are preserved. The phone in image 02 is retained from the source. No generative image model or external API was used. Original photographs and existing site components were not overwritten.
