## Confirmed market and recommended offer

The owner confirmed **small businesses across India** as the target market. Focus the initial offer on owner-operated shops and service businesses that need their first professional website and receive enquiries through WhatsApp or phone. Industry selection remains a hypothesis; geography is confirmed.

Use the existing national service pages for this work. The real Kozhikode address supports business credibility, while the existing Kerala and city pages remain supporting service-area pages. An India-wide strategy does not require a separate page for every Indian city. This research initially covers English search phrases; other languages require customer evidence and an actual content/support capability.

The strongest concrete offer in the current source is a **₹2,000 one-page starter website**, with the first version in **48 hours after the required content is ready**, review before payment, and one focused revision round. Bigger websites, ecommerce and apps have separate quotes. Do not present the starter price or timeline as applying to every service.

Suggested positioning for later copy work:

> A first business website for shops and service providers across India, with clear service information and an easy WhatsApp contact path. One-page starter package: ₹2,000. Review the first version before payment.

The first customer needs to explain an offer and receive enquiries. A business requiring inventory, online payments, customer accounts or booking administration needs a separate scope. Shops and service businesses are the initial audience hypothesis, not proof of which industry will convert best.

The only visible clearly commercial query was `small business website bangalore`, with 2 impressions and no clicks. That is a watch item, not enough evidence to choose Bangalore or forecast demand.

This step reviewed public search results for India-wide small-business services, one-page websites, pricing, landing pages and WhatsApp catalogues, plus supporting local searches. It did **not** measure location-specific Google rankings, inspect a local Maps pack, or obtain Keyword Planner/Ahrefs/Semrush volume and difficulty data. Search results identify competing page types and language; they do not establish monthly demand, ranking difficulty or likely leads.

Priority below means **fit with the current offer and usefulness to a buyer**. Monthly searches, organic difficulty and expected leads remain unknown. No numerical estimates have been invented.

### Business details needed before writing promises

These were not settled by the checked package copy:

- Whether ₹2,000 includes a domain, hosting or any recurring costs, and who pays renewals.
- What support and content changes are included after launch, and for how long.
- What happens if the customer does not approve the first version.
- Whether customers receive source files and own the domain/hosting accounts.
- Which languages the team can support for content, calls and ongoing communication; do not promise nationwide language coverage.

The research can proceed with these unknowns. Final pricing promises must use the owner's actual terms. Do not fill the gaps by copying a competitor's package.

## How to validate the plan

After publishing an approved content change and finishing live tracking verification, record the publication date and manually review Search Console by query and page each week. Use country **India** and start with these query groups: `small business|business website`, `one page|single page`, and `cost|price|pricing|package`. Track branded queries separately and keep `kozhikode|calicut|kerala` as a supporting group. These groups can overlap, so do not add their totals together. Inspect the actual query wording; group labels are only a working aid.

Compare equal reporting periods, allowing for data delays. Check whether relevant non-brand queries reach the intended pages, then review clicks and contact actions. Count received enquiries and qualified leads separately. With the current tiny sample, avoid conclusions from one click or a few position changes. Several pages appearing for related queries is not by itself a reason to merge them.

If volume data becomes available, record the tool, date, geographic target, search network and reporting period for each estimate. Google's [Keyword Planner documentation](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en) explains that volumes depend on settings and that its “competition” metric measures **advertisers**, not organic SEO difficulty. No paid campaign or billing setup is part of this step.

Google's [helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports a clear audience, original evidence and answers that help people complete their task. That is the basis for improving package clarity and work examples rather than producing many keyword pages.
